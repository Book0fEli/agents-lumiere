# Privacy at Lumière Agents

A plain-English summary. The **[full privacy policy](https://agents.lumierepaycheck.org/privacy)** is the one that applies, and it wins if anything here differs.

Operated by Lumière LLC (Connecticut, USA). Questions: hello@lumierepaycheck.org

## What we collect

- **From you:** your name, email, business name and address. Billing details are handled by Stripe; we never see or store card numbers.
- **From your accounting software or files:** open invoices (number, amount, balance, due date) and your customers' business names, contact names and email addresses.
- **For the other agents, only if you use them:**
  - Quote follow-up: quotes you've sent (number, amount, dates, a short description) and your prospective customers' names and emails.
  - Paperwork chaser: your subs' and vendors' contact details and the certificates they send.
  - Receipt chaser: card charges (date, merchant, amount, card name), the people assigned to each card, the receipts they send, and your bookkeeper's email if you add one.
  - Missed-call text-back: callers' phone numbers, the texts exchanged, call times, and a link to voicemails (which stay in the Twilio account behind your number).
  - Review requests: customers' names, emails (and mobile numbers, if you add them) and whether they opened your review link.
- **From your customers:** their replies to reminders, requests, follow-ups and texts.
- **Technical:** sign-in sessions and the basic logs needed to run and secure the service.
- **Website visits:** our public pages use Google Analytics. It isn't loaded on signed-in pages, sign-in links or upload links, and never receives your business data or your customers' data.

## How we use it

Only to run your agents for you, show you results, bill you and keep the service secure.

- **We don't sell data.**
- **We don't use it for advertising.**
- **We don't use your data to train AI models.** When AI words a reminder or reads a reply, only the text needed for that task goes to the AI provider, under terms that don't allow them to train on it.

## Connections

- **Accounting and field service software** (QuickBooks, Jobber, Housecall Pro, FreshBooks, Zoho Books, Wave, Square): read only, never written to. Disconnect any time and we stop accessing your data immediately.
- **Google Sheets and Excel:** read only. A Google Sheet is read only if you share it with us as a Viewer.
- **Gmail and Outlook:** send-only permission ("gmail.send", "Mail.Send"). We can't read, search or delete your email. Lumière Agents' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
- **SMTP mailboxes** (Proton Mail, Zoho and others): the sending password or token is stored encrypted and deleted when you stop using it. We recommend a token made just for Lumière Agents.
- **HubSpot:** we add notes to contacts that already exist, matched by email. We never create contacts or change anything else.
- **Webhooks and the API:** event details go only to the addresses you add, signed. Remove any of them at any time.

## Who we share it with

Only the providers that run the service, each processing data on our behalf: Render (hosting), Resend (email), Stripe (payments), Twilio (texting), AI providers when AI features are used, and Google Analytics for public page visits. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md#who-processes-data-for-us).

## Your customers' choices

Everyone you contact can opt out: an unsubscribe link and STOP in every email, and STOP for texts. Once someone opts out, no agent contacts them again.

## How long we keep it

While your account is open. We delete it within 90 days of your request or of your account closing, except billing records we're required to keep. You can download all of your data at any time.
